## Faults that went through, and what would stop them

- `replayed_row_new_key`: one event re-ingested under a new key. Would be stopped by uniqueness on a natural key or the upstream event id, not only the surrogate key.
- `amount_off_by_10x`: one amount off by a factor of 10. Would be stopped by a per-row comparison with that customer's or product's history, or a batch total vs recent batches.
- `stale_batch`: last year's file reloaded (dates -365 days). Would be stopped by a freshness test: the newest date must be within N days of the load.
